MedPharm Portal — Patient User Guide

A walkthrough of every screen and action available to you in the patient web portal.
Copyright © 2026 Enlightec Ltd. · www.enlightec.com · Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later

1. Overview

The MedPharm Patient Portal is your secure online window into your care. From any browser you can:

  • View your active prescriptions and request refills.
  • See your full medication list with dosing instructions.
  • Read your medical records, lab results, imaging reports, procedures, and referrals.
  • Track upcoming and past appointments.
  • Pay bills online and review payment history.
  • Send secure messages to your providers and read their replies.
  • Keep your contact details, insurance, and emergency contact information up to date.

Everything you do here is mirrored in the same database used by your clinic's staff (the desktop and mobile clinical clients), so a refill request, payment, or message you send appears for your care team in real time.

Tip. The portal is responsive — it works comfortably on phones, tablets, and desktops. On small screens the top navigation collapses behind the icon next to the MedPharm logo.

2. Signing In

The first page you see is the login screen. Enter the username and password you created during registration, then click Sign In.

  • Passwords are checked against a hashed value — your real password is never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • After several failed attempts in a row your account is temporarily locked. The on-screen message tells you how many attempts remain and, if locked, how many minutes to wait.
  • Sessions time out automatically after a period of inactivity. If you walk away from your computer, you'll be returned to the login screen on your next click.
Forgot your password? The portal does not offer self-service password reset. Call your clinic and the front-desk staff will reset it for you.

3. Creating an Account

If you've never used the portal before, click Create Account from the login page. The portal will verify your identity against the patient record your clinic already has on file.

You'll be asked for:

  • First and last name — exactly as the clinic has them.
  • Date of birth.
  • Last 4 digits of your SSN — used only to confirm it's really you. The full SSN is never requested.
  • Username — what you'll log in with.
  • Email — for portal correspondence.
  • Password and confirmation — must meet the minimum length and complexity shown on the page.

If the four identity fields don't match a clinic record, registration is declined. Call the office and ask them to confirm your demographics — a small mismatch (a hyphen, a middle initial) is usually all it takes.

4. Navigating the Portal

Once signed in, every page shares the same layout:

  1. Top navigation bar. The MedPharm logo on the left returns you to the dashboard. The center links take you to each module: Dashboard, Prescriptions, Billing, Records, Appointments, Medications, Messages. On narrow screens these collapse behind the icon.
  2. Notification bell () on the right shows a red badge when something needs attention — see §11 Notifications.
  3. User menu on the far right shows your name with a icon. Clicking it opens the dropdown with Profile, User Guide (this document), and Logout.
  4. Workspace — the main content area where each module displays its content.
  5. Footer — copyright and HIPAA-compliance confirmation.

5. Dashboard

The first screen after sign-in. It summarises everything that needs your attention — active prescriptions, outstanding balances, upcoming appointments, and recent invoices.

5.1 Summary Cards

  • Active Prescriptions — count of prescriptions currently active.
  • Upcoming Appointments — count of scheduled or confirmed visits.
  • Outstanding Balance — total amount due across all unpaid invoices (sent, partial, or overdue).

5.2 Quick Sections

  • Active Prescriptions — a peek at what's currently prescribed; click any row to open the full detail.
  • Upcoming Appointments — the next five visits with date, provider, and reason.
  • Recent Invoices — the five newest bills, with status and balance.

Click any item to jump straight to its detail page. The full lists live under the Prescriptions, Appointments, and Billing menus.

6. Prescriptions & Refills

The Prescriptions page shows every prescription written for you, split into Active and Past tabs. Past includes prescriptions that are filled, cancelled, or expired.

6.1 Reading a Prescription

Click any prescription to open the detail page. You'll see:

  • Header — Rx number, prescriber name and NPI, prescribe date, expiry, and the linked diagnosis (with its ICD-10 code, where one is on file).
  • Medication line items — drug name, dosage, frequency, duration, quantity, refills used and refills allowed, and your prescriber's instructions.
  • Notes — any clinical notes the prescriber attached to the order.

6.2 Requesting a Refill

On a prescription's detail page, click Request Refill. The portal walks every line item with refills remaining and submits a refill request to your clinic. You'll see a green confirmation banner; if no items had refills left you'll see an amber "No refills remaining" message instead.

What happens next. Your refill request appears immediately in your clinic's pharmacy queue. The pharmacy fills it on their next pass — controlled substances (DEA Schedule II–V) may need a fresh prescription instead of a refill, in which case you'll receive a secure message asking you to schedule a quick follow-up.

7. My Medications

The Medications page collapses every active prescription into a single list of drugs you are currently taking, with dosing, frequency, and your prescriber's name next to each one. This is the screen to use as a refresher before a visit, or to share with another provider.

For each medication you'll see:

  • Brand name and generic name.
  • Form (tablet, capsule, liquid, injection…).
  • Strength.
  • Dosage and frequency from your prescription.
  • Refills remaining and instructions.
  • Prescribing provider.
Allergies. If your Profile lists an allergy, the prescribing system flags any new prescription that conflicts with it before your provider signs it. The allergies your clinic has on file are shown on the Profile page — keep them current.

8. Appointments

The Appointments page lists every visit scheduled for you, separated into Upcoming (status scheduled or confirmed) and Past (completed, cancelled, or no-show).

Each row shows date and time, provider, type, status, and reason.

8.1 Scheduling, Cancelling, Rescheduling

Appointment scheduling is handled by your clinic's front-desk staff. To book, change, or cancel an appointment, send a secure message (see §10) or call the office. Your clinic's actions appear on this page in real time.

9. Medical Records

The Records page shows every clinical record your clinic has shared with you. Use the Type filter at the top to narrow by:

TypeWhat it contains
Lab ResultsBlood work, urinalysis, lipid panel, etc., including the lab's reference ranges.
ImagingX-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound reports — the radiologist's narrative.
ProceduresIn-clinic procedures performed during a visit (sutures, injections, biopsies, EKG).
ReferralsOutgoing referrals to a specialist.
OtherFree-form clinical notes that don't fit the categories above.

Each record shows its title, type, the author, the date it was created, and the body content. Records are read-only — if you spot something that looks wrong, message your provider and they will correct it from their side.

What's not shown here. Some clinics deliberately hold sensitive results (genetic tests, mental health notes, and similar) out of the portal until a provider has reviewed them with you in person. If you're expecting a result that isn't visible yet, that's why.

10. Bills & Payments

The Billing page consolidates your financial relationship with the clinic.

10.1 Outstanding Balance

The card at the top shows the total you owe across all unpaid invoices. This is the same number that appears on the dashboard.

10.2 Invoice List

Every invoice you've been issued, with status:

  • Draft — drawn up by your clinic but not yet released.
  • Sent — released and awaiting payment.
  • Partial — partly paid, with a remaining balance.
  • Paid — settled in full.
  • Overdue — past the due date and still unpaid.
  • Cancelled — voided by the clinic.

10.3 Paying a Bill

Click Pay next to an unpaid invoice. The payment page lets you enter:

  • Amount — defaults to the full balance; you can pay less for a partial payment.
  • Payment method — credit card, debit card, or online (ACH).

On a successful payment the balance recalculates instantly. If the balance reaches zero, the invoice transitions to Paid; otherwise it becomes Partial.

Receipts. Each successful payment is recorded with a transaction reference, which you can review in Payment History below the invoice list. Save that page or take a screenshot for your records.

10.4 Payment History

Below the invoices, you'll find a chronological list of every payment you've made through the portal — date, amount, method, and reference number. This is the page your accountant will probably want a print of.

11. Secure Messages

The Messages page is your direct line to your care team. It is HIPAA-compliant and end-to-end tracked, so it's the right channel for anything more clinical than a scheduling question.

11.1 The Inbox

Each row is one conversation thread, showing the subject, the provider, the timestamp of the most recent message, and an unread indicator if there's a reply you haven't read yet.

11.2 Starting a Thread

Click + New Message. Pick the provider (optional — leave blank if you don't know who handles your question and the clinic will route it), type a subject, write the body, and click Send. The new thread appears at the top of your inbox.

11.3 Reading and Replying

Click any thread to open the full conversation. The reply box at the bottom is for sending a follow-up — write, click Send, and your message is delivered instantly. The portal marks every incoming message read as soon as you open the thread.

Not for emergencies. Secure messages are read during clinic hours. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) — do not send a portal message.

Threads can be closed by either side once a question is resolved. A closed thread stays in your inbox and is searchable, but it can no longer accept replies — if you need to follow up, start a new thread.

12. My Profile

The Profile page (in the user dropdown, top right) shows the personal information your clinic has on file. Some sections are display-only; others are editable from here.

12.1 Personal Information

Read-only — name, date of birth, gender, blood type. If any of this is wrong, ask the clinic to correct it; this data affects clinical decisions and is changed in person.

12.2 Contact Information

Editable. Update your email, phone, address, city, state, and ZIP, then click Save. Your update propagates immediately to the clinic's records.

12.3 Insurance

Read-only summary of the insurance carrier(s) the clinic has on file for billing. To swap insurance or add a new policy, send a secure message with the new card details (front and back), or call the office.

12.4 Allergies

Read-only list of allergens, severity, and reaction the clinic has recorded for you. The prescribing system warns your provider if a candidate medication conflicts with anything on this list — so keeping it accurate is important. Ask your provider to update it during your next visit, or send a secure message.

13. Notifications

The bell in the top-right navigation shows a red badge when something needs attention. The badge counts:

  • Overdue invoices — flagged in red.
  • Outstanding invoices with a remaining balance — flagged in amber.
  • Upcoming appointments — informational reminders.

Clicking the bell shows the most recent ten notifications. The list refreshes automatically while the page is open.

14. User Menu

Click your name in the top-right corner of the navigation bar to open the user menu. It contains:

ItemWhat it does
ProfileOpens your profile page (see §12).
User GuideOpens this document.
LogoutEnds your session and returns you to the login screen. Always log out when you're finished, especially on a shared computer.

15. Privacy & Security

The portal is built to comply with the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Practical things that means for you:

  • Encryption in transit. Every page is served over TLS — look for the padlock in your browser's address bar.
  • Hashed passwords. Your password is stored as a one-way hash. Even the clinic's IT staff can't read it.
  • Session timeout. If you walk away from your computer, your session ends automatically and you'll need to log back in.
  • Brute-force protection. Repeated failed logins lock the account temporarily.
  • CSRF tokens. Every form submission carries a one-time token, so links from outside the portal can't act on your behalf.
  • Audit log. Every action you take and every message you read is logged for compliance.

Your clinic's responsibilities (Business Associate Agreement with the platform vendor, breach-notification policy, and so on) are documented separately — ask the office for their Notice of Privacy Practices.

16. Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely cause & what to try
"Invalid username or password" on a password I'm sure is correct Check Caps Lock. Usernames are case-insensitive but passwords are case-sensitive. After several failed attempts, the account locks for a few minutes — wait, then try again, or call the office to reset.
"Too many failed login attempts" Wait the number of minutes shown on the screen, then sign in again. The cool-down resets the counter.
Login works but I'm immediately back at the login page Your browser is blocking cookies. Allow cookies for this site (or remove an aggressive privacy extension), then try again.
Can't see a recent lab result Some clinics hold sensitive results until a provider has reviewed them with you. Send a secure message if you've been waiting more than a few business days.
Refill button is greyed out / not visible The prescription has no refills remaining, or the medication is a controlled substance that requires a fresh prescription. Send a secure message asking your prescriber.
Payment was charged but the invoice still shows a balance Refresh the page; if the balance still hasn't updated, check Payment History for the transaction reference and forward it to the office in a secure message.
Couldn't verify identity at registration One of the four fields (name, DOB, last 4 of SSN) doesn't match the clinic's record. Call the office to confirm what they have on file.
Page looks broken / styles missing Hard-refresh: Ctrl+F5 on Windows / Linux, or Cmd+Shift+R on macOS. Your browser may be serving a cached copy from before a portal upgrade.
