# Business Associate Agreement — Template

**This template is a starting point, not legal advice.** Have counsel
review and tailor the final agreement before executing.

---

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
**{effective_date}** between **{covered_entity_name}** ("Covered Entity")
and **{business_associate_name}** ("Business Associate") operating the
MedPharm ERP deployment at **{deployment_url}**.

## 1. Definitions

Terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given
them in the HIPAA Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and
Enforcement Rules at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, the "HIPAA
Rules").

## 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

Business Associate may use or disclose Protected Health Information
("PHI") only:

- as necessary to perform the services set forth in the underlying
  service agreement;
- as required by law (45 CFR § 164.504(e)(2)(i));
- for the proper management and administration of Business Associate or
  to carry out its legal responsibilities.

## 3. Safeguards

Business Associate will implement appropriate administrative, physical,
and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the PHI that it creates,
receives, maintains, or transmits, in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.308,
§ 164.310, and § 164.312. The technical safeguards implemented by
MedPharm ERP are documented in `docs/HIPAA_COMPLIANCE.md`.

## 4. Subcontractors

Business Associate will ensure that any subcontractor that creates,
receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf agrees in writing to
the same restrictions and conditions that apply through this Agreement.

## 5. Reporting

Business Associate will report to Covered Entity:

- Any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement
  **within 5 business days** of discovery.
- Any Security Incident, including any Breach of Unsecured PHI as
  defined by 45 CFR § 164.402, **without unreasonable delay and in no
  event later than 30 calendar days** after discovery.

## 6. Access and Amendment

Business Associate will, within 15 business days of a written request,
provide Covered Entity with access to PHI in a Designated Record Set and
will incorporate amendments as directed (45 CFR § 164.524, § 164.526).

## 7. Accounting of Disclosures

Business Associate will document disclosures of PHI and information
related to such disclosures as would be required for Covered Entity to
respond to a request for an accounting under 45 CFR § 164.528.
MedPharm's hash-chained audit log and PHI Access Log satisfy the
technical logging requirement.

## 8. Termination

- **For cause.** Either party may terminate for a material breach
  uncured 30 days after written notice.
- **Effect of termination.** Upon termination, Business Associate will,
  if feasible, return or destroy all PHI; if not feasible, the
  protections of this Agreement extend to that PHI indefinitely.

## 9. Miscellaneous

This Agreement is governed by the law of **{governing_state}**. The
parties intend this Agreement to be interpreted to permit compliance
with the HIPAA Rules; in case of ambiguity, an interpretation consistent
with compliance controls.
