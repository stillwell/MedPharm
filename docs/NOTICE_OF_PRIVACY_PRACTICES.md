## Our pledge regarding your health information

We are required by federal law (the Health Insurance Portability and
Accountability Act, "HIPAA") and applicable state law to maintain the
privacy of your **Protected Health Information** ("PHI"), to give you
this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow
the terms of this Notice currently in effect.

## How we may use and disclose your PHI

### Without your written authorisation

We may use or disclose your PHI for the following purposes without
asking for your specific permission, because the law allows us to:

- **Treatment.** To provide, coordinate, or manage your health care
  with you and other providers (for example, sharing your medication
  list with the pharmacist who fills a prescription, or sending lab
  results to a specialist we refer you to).
- **Payment.** To bill and collect payment for services — submitting
  claims to your insurer, verifying coverage, and following up on
  unpaid balances.
- **Health-care operations.** Internal activities such as quality
  assessment, accreditation, training of staff and students, business
  planning, and customer service.
- **Required by law.** Public-health activities (communicable-disease
  reporting, FDA adverse-event reporting, child-abuse reporting),
  judicial and administrative proceedings, law-enforcement requests
  that meet the regulatory criteria, organ-and-tissue donation,
  research approved by an Institutional Review Board, workers'
  compensation, and disclosures to coroners or funeral directors.
- **To avert a serious threat** to your health or safety, or that of
  another person or the public, when consistent with applicable law
  and ethical standards.
- **Health-oversight activities.** Audits, investigations, and
  inspections by oversight agencies, including HHS for HIPAA
  enforcement.

### Only with your written authorisation

We will obtain your written authorisation before:

- Using or disclosing **psychotherapy notes** kept separately from your
  medical record.
- Using or disclosing PHI for **marketing**, except a face-to-face
  communication or a promotional gift of nominal value.
- **Selling** PHI.
- Any **other use or disclosure** not described in this Notice or
  permitted by law.

You may revoke a written authorisation in writing at any time. We
cannot undo disclosures already made in reliance on the prior
authorisation.

## Your rights regarding your PHI

You have the following rights, which you can exercise by contacting
the Privacy Officer named at the bottom of this Notice. Most requests
must be in writing on a form we will provide.

| Right | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Access** (§ 164.524) | Request a copy of your PHI in our designated record set, in the format you request if readily producible. We respond within 30 days; one 30-day extension is permitted with written notice. We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee for copies. |
| **Amendment** (§ 164.526) | Ask us to correct PHI you believe is incorrect or incomplete. We will respond within 60 days; one 30-day extension is permitted. We may deny the request in limited circumstances; you may file a written statement of disagreement. |
| **Accounting of disclosures** (§ 164.528) | Receive a list of disclosures we have made of your PHI in the previous six years (excluding disclosures for treatment, payment, or operations, those you authorised, and certain others). The first accounting in any 12-month period is free. |
| **Restrictions** (§ 164.522(a)) | Request a restriction on certain uses or disclosures. We are not required to agree, except for disclosures to a health plan when you have paid in full out of pocket for the service. |
| **Confidential communications** (§ 164.522(b)) | Request that we communicate with you about health matters in a particular way or at a particular location (for example, only at your work address, only by encrypted email). |
| **Paper copy of this Notice** | Receive a paper copy at any time, even if you have agreed to receive it electronically. |
| **Notification of breach** (§ 164.404) | Be notified, in writing, in the event of a breach of unsecured PHI affecting you. |
| **Choose someone to act for you** | Designate a personal representative who may exercise your rights and make health-care decisions for you, supported by appropriate documentation. |

## Patient Web Portal — special points

If you use our patient web portal:

- You log in with a unique username and password. The system locks
  the account temporarily after several failed attempts.
- We do not store your password — only a one-way cryptographic hash.
- All portal traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or newer).
- Your sessions time out automatically after a period of inactivity.
- Messages you send through the portal travel only between you and
  our staff; they are not transmitted via standard email.

You may stop using the portal at any time by asking the front desk to
disable your account.

## Our duties

We are required by law to:

- Maintain the privacy and security of your PHI.
- Provide you with this Notice describing our legal duties and
  privacy practices.
- Abide by the terms of this Notice currently in effect.
- Notify you in writing if a breach occurs that may compromise the
  privacy or security of your PHI.

## Changes to this Notice

We reserve the right to revise this Notice and to make the revised
version effective for PHI we already have as well as PHI we receive in
the future. The current Notice is always posted at our office, on our
public website, and on the patient portal. Material changes will be
re-distributed through the portal and at the front desk.
